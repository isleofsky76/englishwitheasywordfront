1. 해 달라고 부탁하다

2. 공식적으로 요청하다

Visitors are requested not to take photos.

3. 도움·허락 등을 구하다

He sought advice from his teacher.

4. 간청[요청]하다

The company solicited feedback from customers.

5. 진정[탄원/청원]하다

Residents petitioned the city to build a park.

6. 간청하다

She pleaded with him to stay.

7. 간청[애원]하다

He entreated her to forgive him.

8. 애원하다

The child begged for one more story.

9. 애원[간청]하다

He implored the judge for mercy.

10. 간청하다, 애원하다

I beseech you to listen to me.

11. 간절히 원하다, 애원[탄원]하다

They supplicated the king for mercy.

12. 성가시게 조르다

The kids pestered their dad for a puppy.

13. 계속 들볶다

Reporters badgered her with questions.

14. 집요하게 졸라대다

He importuned her for money again and again.

15. 공개적으로 요구하다

Experts are calling for stricter rules.

16. (큰 소리로) 요구하다

Fans clamored for an encore.

17. 고집하며 요구하다

He insisted on paying the bill.

18. 강하게 요구하다

The protesters demanded his resignation.
